# 📊 E-Commerce Analysis Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive **Power BI E-Commerce Analysis Dashboard** designed to analyze sales, profit, customers, orders, regional performance, category performance, and shipping efficiency.

The dashboard helps transform e-commerce data into actionable business insights through interactive visuals, KPIs, filters, and detailed performance analysis.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Understand performance across product categories.
- Compare regional sales, profit, and orders.
- Analyze shipping costs and their impact on profitability.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming categories and regions.
- Provide actionable business insights for profitable growth.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### Overall Performance

Sales and profit are concentrated in a smaller number of categories. **Food and Disposables** are strong contributors to both sales and profit.

### Regional Performance

The **East region** is the strongest market, followed by Central. The unusually low contribution from the **Other** region requires further review.

### Shipping Efficiency

High shipping costs may reduce profitability, especially for categories with high shipment volumes. **Food and Supplements** should be reviewed for packaging, weight, order size, and shipping-rate efficiency.

---

## 🔎 Key Takeaways

- Focus on **profitable growth**.
- Improve **regional balance**.
- Reduce unnecessary **shipping costs**.
- Review weaker-performing markets.
- Optimize shipping for high-cost categories.
- Reduce dependency on a limited number of regions and categories.
- Continue strengthening Food and Disposables, which are major sales and profit drivers.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the **Region** and **Category** slicers to filter the dashboard.
4. Explore sales, profit, orders, customers, and shipping performance.
5. Use the visualizations to identify business trends and opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Strengthen the East region** while identifying opportunities in weaker regions.
2. **Optimize Food and Disposables**, which are important sales and profit contributors.
3. **Review shipping costs** for high-volume categories.
4. Investigate the unusually low contribution from the **Other** region.
5. Analyze state and category-level performance to identify specific profit drivers.
6. Focus on sustainable and profitable growth rather than sales growth alone.
